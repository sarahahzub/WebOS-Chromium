The real Chromium browser for your LG TV

Chromium with the full Chrome interface, running natively on webOS: tabs, address bar, menus, bookmarks, history, downloads and extensions from the Chrome Web Store. No root needed.

Features

  • Native: built for the TV's own processor, display server and GPU driver. No emulation.
  • GPU rendering on the TV's graphics chip.
  • Sharp 1080p: drawn at the TV's full resolution with a large interface.
  • Hardware video decoding for every video on every site, up to 1080p60.
  • Remote control: arrows, OK and Back move through pages, menus and tabs. The Magic Remote pointer works as a mouse, and LG's on-screen keyboard is used for typing.
  • TV Tools (built in, in the toolbar): video quality limit for any site (up to 4K, which may stutter at the start), TV versions of websites, lite mode, tab limit and background tab unloading.
  • About 140 MB of memory when idle.

Requirements

Needs webOS 9 (webOS 24) or newer. Check Settings > Support > TV Information > webOS TV Version. On older TVs (webOS 8 and below) the app shows a message and closes; support for them may come later.

Good to know

  • Tested on webOS 10 (LG 43UR7500PSC). Other models: please report how it works on the project page.
  • No Netflix or other DRM video, and no Google account sync.
